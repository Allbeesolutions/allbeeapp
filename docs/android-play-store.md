## Important Play App Signing step

The fingerprint above is the local upload/release certificate. Google Play App Signing normally uses a different app-signing certificate for the APKs delivered to users. After the app is created/enrolled in Play Console, obtain the **App signing key certificate SHA-256** from Play Console and add that fingerprint to `public/.well-known/assetlinks.json` alongside the local fingerprint. This is required for verified App Links/TWA association on Play-distributed builds.

Google's current guidance confirms that the Play Console app-signing fingerprint should be used when Play App Signing is enabled.
